  • Ability to work outdoors in varying weather conditions.

Ironworker

Central Alabama | Field Position
Inquire About Availability
Suggested Starting Pay $23.00 – $29.00 / Hour Proposed range only. Actual compensation must be confirmed by MBBuilders Inc.

Position Overview

Perform structural steel erection, pre-engineered metal building assembly, and installation of metal roof and wall systems on commercial and industrial projects. Work with field crews to complete erection activities safely and efficiently.

Key Responsibilities

  • Erect and connect structural steel components.
  • Install primary and secondary metal building framing.
  • Install metal roofing and wall panels.
  • Assist with rigging and crane operations.
  • Operate aerial lifts and other equipment when qualified and authorized.
  • Perform cutting, fitting, and minor field fabrication.
  • Follow fall protection, rigging, and jobsite safety requirements.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous ironworking or metal erection experience.
  • Knowledge of structural steel and metal building systems.
  • Ability to work safely at heights.
  • Experience with construction tools and equipment.
  • Rigging and equipment operation experience.
  • Ability to read construction drawings is a plus.
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills.

Potential Benefits

  • Paid time off.
  • Paid holidays.
  • Separate paid sick leave.
  • Opportunities for overtime.
  • Equipment and safety training.
  • Opportunities for advancement into crew leadership.

Interested in this position? Contact MBBuilders Inc. for availability and application details.

Project Manager

Central Alabama | Management Position
Inquire About Availability
Suggested Starting Salary $30.00 - $35.00 / Hour Proposed range only. Actual compensation must be confirmed by MBBuilders Inc.

Position Overview

Manage and coordinate commercial and industrial metal erection projects from planning through completion. Maintain communication with field crews, suppliers, customers, and general contractors while supporting project schedules, quality standards, and financial performance.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and coordinate construction schedules.
  • Communicate with general contractors, suppliers, and customers.
  • Coordinate manpower, materials, and company equipment.
  • Monitor project progress and job costs.
  • Review construction drawings and project specifications.
  • Assist with change orders, project documentation, and reporting.
  • Support company safety and quality standards.
  • Help ensure projects are completed on schedule.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Construction management experience preferred.
  • Experience with structural steel or metal buildings is a plus.
  • Strong organizational and communication skills.
  • Ability to read and interpret construction drawings.
  • Knowledge of construction scheduling and project coordination.
  • Proficiency with common computer applications.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple project responsibilities.

Potential Benefits

  • Paid time off.
  • Paid holidays.
  • Separate paid sick leave.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • Leadership advancement opportunities.
  • Potential performance-based compensation.

Interested in this position? Contact MBBuilders Inc. for availability and application details.
